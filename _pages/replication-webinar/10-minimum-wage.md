## Session details

**Speaker:** Oscar Jaramillo (Torcuato de Tella University)  
**Date:** December 1, 2022

## Abstract

We discuss the results so far and how to continue with the replication of the study “Can the minimum wage reduce poverty and inequality in the developing world? Evidence from Brazil”, Orlando J. Sotomayor (2021), *World Development* 138, DOI: [10.1016/j.worlddev.2020.105182](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.worlddev.2020.105182).

## Bio

Oscar Jaramillo is an economist from Central University of Ecuador, currently studying for a Master's degree in economics at Torcuato de Tella University in Argentina.
